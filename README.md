# customer-insights-disclosure-design
Exploratory customer-review analysis of ChatGPT and BRITA, with proposed A/B experiments on ongoing-use disclosure.
# Customer Insights and Ongoing-Use Disclosure

An independent portfolio project connecting customer-review analysis with communication design and proposed online A/B experiments.

**Status:** Exploratory review analysis and research mock-ups completed. The researcher checked the sampled review texts and ratings against their source pages. Experiments are proposed, not conducted; implementation details remain to be finalised.

## Project question

How do customers describe whether a product's continuing benefits justify its ongoing requirements, and how can these accounts inform clearer purchase information?

The project examines two contexts:

- A paid ChatGPT subscription, involving access conditions and usage restrictions.
- A BRITA Marella filter jug, involving cartridge replacement and ongoing expense.

The products are analysed separately. This is not a comparison of which brand performs better.

## Review analysis

The working sample contains 20 public reviews:

- 10 reviews explicitly referring to paid ChatGPT use, collected from Trustpilot.
- 10 reviews concerning a BRITA Marella jug, collected from a B&Q product-review page.

Reviews were collected newest-first among eligible written reviews. The researcher checked the review texts and ratings against the linked sources and confirmed the BRITA reviews appeared on the selected product listing. Relative review dates are retained as displayed.

Each review was documented and coded for:

- Expected benefits or conditions, distinguishing explicit statements from inference.
- Reported experiences.
- Descriptive issue and benefit codes.
- Interpretation notes and uncertainty.

Related codes were grouped into broader themes. Review IDs link each theme to its supporting evidence.

Counts represent distinct reviews mentioning a theme. A review may support several themes, so counts overlap.

Initial coding and synthesis were AI-assisted. Researcher review of the coding decisions is required before treating the analysis as final.

## Findings within the sample

### ChatGPT

| Theme | Reviews |
|---|---:|
| Support and problem resolution | 5 |
| Dependability for completing tasks | 4 |
| Financial value of the subscription | 4 |
| Predictability of paid access | 3 |
| Control over subscription and payments | 2 |
| Control and continuity of work | 2 |

Some reviewers acknowledged useful capabilities while describing uncertainty about access or dissatisfaction with the paid experience.

**Selected insight:** Useful capabilities may feel less valuable when customers cannot anticipate how access restrictions could affect their intended work.

### BRITA

| Theme | Reviews |
|---|---:|
| Everyday handling and design | 5 |
| Cartridge lifespan | 4 |
| Improved taste | 3 |
| Ongoing filter expense | 2 |
| Replacement-cartridge availability | 1 |
| Product fault and troubleshooting | 1 |

Taste improvements sometimes coexisted with concerns about replacement expense. Approximately monthly replacement appeared in both favourable and critical accounts.

**Selected insight:** An appreciated benefit can coexist with concerns about the effort and expense required to maintain it.

## Interpretation

The shared working theme is **maintaining value over time**.

These accounts suggest that customers may evaluate both the benefit and the conditions required to continue receiving it. This is a tentative interpretation, not a demonstrated psychological mechanism.

The reviews do not establish that website presentation caused dissatisfaction. The project therefore uses the findings to generate communication hypotheses rather than diagnose a proven design failure.

## Proposed online experiments

Two separate randomised, between-subjects A/B experiments would evaluate the research mock-ups.

| Product | Version A | Version B |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Benefits with a small limits notice below them. | The same benefits, with an added explanation of usage conditions beside them in equally prominent text. |
| BRITA | Original benefits and cartridge-duration information. | The same content, with added replacement guidance and an example refill expense inside the features section. |

### What the interventions test

The ChatGPT experiment tests a combined change in explanatory content and visual prominence. It does not isolate placement, font size or information detail.

The BRITA experiment tests an added disclosure package. It does not isolate replacement guidance from expense information or additional text.

Version A materials are screenshot-inspired adaptations, not exact reproductions of official pages or verified pages previously seen by reviewers.

### Assignment and procedure

Within each experiment, participants would be assigned through simple randomisation, with a 50% probability of each version.

Each participant would see one product and one version only.

Proposed procedure:

1. Consent and eligibility.
2. Relevant prior-experience questions.
3. Random assignment and panel exposure.
4. Panel removal.
5. Factual comprehension questions.
6. Exploratory secondary ratings.
7. Self-reported noticeability question.

### Hypotheses and measures

**H0:** Mean comprehension scores are equal between versions.

**H1:** Mean comprehension scores differ, with higher comprehension predicted for Version B.

The primary outcome would be a three-question factual comprehension score, ranging from 0 to 3.

Exploratory secondary outcomes:

- Perceived predictability.
- Perceived transparency.
- Confidence in assessing product fit.
- Perceived value.
- Purchase intention.

Noticeability would be self-reported. No eye-tracking or direct visual-attention measurement is included.

The proposed primary analysis estimates the difference in mean comprehension scores, B minus A, with a two-sided 95% confidence interval, separately for each product.

### Remaining implementation work

- Finalise comprehension questions and factual answer keys.
- Verify source details and refresh product facts and prices.
- Pretest readability and question difficulty.
- Determine sample size through a justified power calculation.
- Prespecify exclusions, missing-data handling and statistical methods.

No participants have been recruited and no experimental results are available.

## Limitations

- The small review samples are exploratory and nonrepresentative.
- All selected ChatGPT reviews are one-star, while BRITA ratings are mixed.
- Products, platforms, review periods and subscription plans differ.
- Reviewer claims are not independently verified product facts.
- Review frequencies do not estimate prevalence among all customers.
- The experiments cannot identify the separate effects of every design change.
- Perceived predictability is not objectively predictable access or expense.
- Purchase intention is not an observed purchase.
- No improvements in sales, retention or customer satisfaction have been demonstrated.



The advertising project examines observed performance patterns. This project examines customer accounts and proposes a communication test. Their datasets are separate and do not establish a causal connection.
